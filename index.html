FAQs

Frequently Asked Cleaning & Flooring Questions

Understanding the basics of professional cleaning services can save you time and stress.

As a professional cleaning company, we offer a range of services including:

  • Standard cleaning: Regular dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and surface wiping.
  • Deep cleaning: Intensive cleaning of hard-to-reach areas, appliances, and fixtures.
  • Move-in/move-out cleaning: Thorough cleaning to prepare a property for new occupants.
  • Specialty cleaning: Carpet cleaning, window washing, or upholstery care.
  • Declutter surfaces: Clear countertops, desks, and floors to allow easy access.
  • Secure valuables: Store important documents and valuables safely.
  • Communicate priorities: Let the cleaning team know if there are specific areas needing special attention.
  • Provide access: Ensure the cleaners can enter your property easily.

Trust is our top priority. J.A. Quality addresses this through:

  • Background checks: All employees are thoroughly screened.
  • Training: Ongoing education on cleaning techniques and customer service.
  • Insurance: We carry liability insurance to protect clients and workers.
  • References and reviews: Transparent testimonials from satisfied customers.
  • Hardwood: Timeless beauty and durability in many species and finishes.
  • Laminate: Mimics wood or stone at a lower cost; durable and easy to install.
  • Vinyl: Versatile, water-resistant, available in planks, tiles, and sheets.
  • Tile: Ideal for high-moisture areas like bathrooms and kitchens.
  • Carpet: Adds warmth and comfort to bedrooms and living areas.
  • Weekly or biweekly: Ideal for busy households or high-traffic businesses.
  • Monthly: Suitable for less frequent maintenance or vacation homes.
  • One-time or special occasions: For deep cleans or event preparation.

Regular cleaning helps maintain a healthy environment and prolongs the life of furnishings and surfaces.

Installation time depends on several factors: material type, room size, preparation work needed, and installation method. On average, a professional installer can complete a standard room (200–300 sq ft) in 1–3 days. Complex patterns or multiple rooms may extend the timeline.
